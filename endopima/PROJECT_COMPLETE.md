## ✅ What we can honestly say about it

- It **works**: screening, safety flags, passport, navigation, costs, observatory, village USSD sim, CHP workflow, Dada chat, breathing pause, resume, live EN⇄SW, reduced-motion support — all validated on-device logic.
- It **stays honest**: every demo number is labelled; the score is a symptom-priority indicator, never a diagnosis; no live integrations are claimed; nothing leaves the device unless the user connects cloud sync.
- It **costs nothing to run**: one file, no server, free hosting.

## ⚠️ What we honestly cannot say — and why the review kits are in the archive

The one thing no amount of building today could honestly change: **the clinical logic (questions, weights, bands) has not been reviewed by Kenyan clinicians, and the app has not been systematically field-tested with users.** That is real work that requires other people (professionals, participants, an ethics committee) and real time. Skipping it is fine for a labelled prototype — it is not fine for anything more. If this project is ever taken further, `NEXT_STEPS.md` + the three review kits show exactly the path, with references and timelines already written.

Until then: **the project is done.** Well-built, honestly labelled, and live on the internet.
