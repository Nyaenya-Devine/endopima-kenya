# EndoPima — CHP Field Usability Kit (V1.0)

**Goal:** find out (quickly, cheaply) how Community Health Promoters (CHPs) and young women actually use EndoPima in Nairobi — before any pilot.
**What this is NOT:** a clinical study, a validation of the screener, or a test of the participants. Nothing here produces health claims.
**Companion document:** `review/Pima_Review_Kit.md` — run the clinical review *first* (the instrument should be revised before you take it into the field). This kit is for the *interaction*: tapping, language, flow, comprehension, comfort.

---

## 1. Setup

| Item | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Participants | **3 citizens:** 2–3 CHPs (women preferred; at least one under 40 who uses a smartphone) · **6–8 users:** young women 18–25 who have period pain (a second session for 16–17-year-olds **only with guardian consent + their assent**) |
| Location | Quiet, familiar place — a CHP's workplace, a youth centre, a church/mosque hall. Not a clinic corridor. |
| Device | The team's own phone or tablet (Android preferred; the app is a single HTML file — load it from the local file or a private link). A second person **takes notes; the participant holds the device**. |
| Time | 45–60 min per session. 3 sessions total is enough for a first pass. |
| Payment | Small transport/tea reimbursement is standard and fair; **never** condition help on participation. |
| Recording | Only with explicit permission. Photos/videos of faces are optional — the default is notes only. |

**Ethics reminders (say these out loud at the start):**
- “This is a **test of the app, not of you.** There are no wrong answers.”
- “The app cannot diagnose you. It does not replace a clinician.”
- “You can stop or skip anything, at any time, for any reason.”
- The note-taker never comments on the participant's health. If a participant reports **severe pain, fever + pelvic pain, or very heavy bleeding with dizziness** — the session pauses and the facilitator *privately* gives the “go to the nearest facility now” guidance (the safety check in the app says the same thing).
- Under-18s: guardian consent before the session; the session is stopped if the girl is visibly uncomfortable.

## 2. Session script (bilingual)

**Opening (EN/SW)**
> “We're building an app to help women notice symptoms earlier and find the right care. We want to see what's easy and what's confusing — so we're watching people use it and asking a few questions. Do you have ~45 minutes? Everything you say here stays here.”
> “Tunajenga app ya kusaidia wanawake kutambua dalili mapema na kupata huduma sahihi. Tunataka kuona ni nini rahisi na nini kinachochanganya — kwa hiyo tunaangalia watu wakitumia na kuuliza maswali machache. Una dakika ~45? Kila unachosema kinabaki hapa.”

**Warm-up (2 min):** show the app on the study device. “This is a demo — the numbers in it are made up; the features are simulated. Nothing you do here is sent anywhere.”

**Written consent for observation:** one short form (or verbal consent recorded in notes) — purpose, what is observed, that it is voluntary, that no health data is collected beyond what the user types, and contact for questions.

## 3. Task list (hand one task at a time; do NOT demo first)

| # | Task (say it; don't point) | Success looks like | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | “Find where you would check your symptoms.” | Taps the right button within ~30 s | Does she understand the hero? Is “Pima” meaningful? |
| T2 | “Start the check, then answer the first 4 questions by yourself.” | Reaches Q5 in < 2 min | Reads options? Confusion on any question/words? Does she read aloud? |
| T3 | “Stop in the middle. Come back later. Continue where you left off.” | Resume box appears; Continue restores | Does she notice the resume box? Does “Start over” scare her (it warns!) |
| T4 | “Switch the app to Kiswahili, then answer 2 more questions.” | Language toggle works; questions in SW | Any leftover English? Do the SW words make sense? |
| T5 | “Go to Village Mode and use it like a phone menu (1, 2, 3…).” | Reaches a topic; back to menu | Is the number menu intuitive? Does she press the keypad? |
| T6 | “Open the Dada chat and tap one of the quick buttons.” | Chip → reply appears | Does she know chips are tappable? |
| T7 | “Find where your health story / passport would live, and where you'd find help for costs.” | Finds Thibiti + Fanya | Is the naming (kipi?) confusing? |
| T8 | “Finish the check. What does the result tell you to do?” | Reads band + action correctly | Does she understand HIGH vs URGENT? Does she think it's a diagnosis? (If yes → **critical finding**) |

*Behind each task: record completion (done / done with help / not done), time, errors, and any exact words she struggled with. The note-taker writes **what she said**, verbatim.*

## 5. Debrief (10 min, relaxed)

1. What was the easiest thing you did just now? / Nini kilikuwa rahisi zaidi?
2. What was confusing or annoying? / Nini kilichochanganya?
3. Would you use this on your own phone? What would make you trust it more?
4. (CHPs only) Would you use this in your work? What would your supervisor need to approve it?
5. Is there anything you'd want to ask the app that it doesn't ask? / Kuna kitu ungependa kuuliza app ambayo haiulizi?

## 6. Distress & safeguarding

- Pre-agreed signal with the note-taker; break the session; private space; facilitator (not the app) gives care guidance; offer the nearest facility / hotline info; never push; log anything unusual (with permission only).

## 7. Reporting template (fill per session, 1 page)

- Date / venue / participant type (CHP n=__; users n=__)
- Tasks failed or needing help (count per task)
- Top 5 verbatim quotes (raw, untranslated)
- Top issues ranked: **P0 = blocks use or risks a misunderstanding**; P1 = confusing; P2 = polish
- Unexpected surprises
- Recommended changes → turn each into a one-line app ticket (e.g., “rename X to Y”, “add SW word Z”)

## 8. Expected output & decision rule

- **If no P0 and ≤ 3 P1 issues** → the app can move to a supervised county pilot discussion with the review kit's final version.
- **If P0 exists** → fix, then re-run one session before any pilot talk.
